Peace. Quiet. Dead flowers. That was the plan.

Then the rainbow cousins arrived, because apparently every family has a committee for ruining your vibe.

Their attacks don't kill you. Worse: they make you pretty. Let the Beauty meter fill and congratulations, you're sparkly now. Game over, emotionally and mechanically.

Tap or click toward a cousin to throw Gloom bolts. Get close and hold to drain the color out of them like a responsible adult. Fill VOID, hit BLACKOUT, and enjoy one blessed second where the world remembers black is a color too.

Between fights, scrub the rainbow residue off the floor. Nobody else is going to do it.

Mobile-first, 13KB, no external assets. The pixels and noises are generated in code at runtime because shipping tiny is a lifestyle choice.

Made for js13kGames 2026: Unicorns and Rainbows.
